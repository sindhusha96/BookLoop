Simple & Safe

How BookLoop Works

Everything you need to buy, sell, or exchange textbooks with fellow students in 3 painless steps.

Step 1
List Your Course Books

Snap a cover photo, pick condition, specify your department and semester, and choose whether you want cash, an exchange, or either.

Step 2
Send or Accept Requests

Browse course books from students on campus. Send purchase requests or offer one of your own books in an exchange swap.

Step 3
Meet & Hand Over

Once accepted, student contact details are securely revealed. Meet up at your campus library or student union to complete the exchange.

Why Students Love BookLoop

Meeting campus peers to swap books often leads to course advice, exam tips, and great campus connections.
