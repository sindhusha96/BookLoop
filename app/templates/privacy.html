Privacy Policy & Safe Exchange Guidelines

Last Updated: Academic Year 2026
1. Student Contact Privacy

At BookLoop, we treat student privacy with utmost seriousness. Your personal contact phone number and direct email address are never shown publicly on listings or catalog browse pages. Your contact details are only disclosed to another registered student once you mutually accept an exchange or purchase request.

2. Direct Peer-to-Peer Model

BookLoop operates as an information matching platform between college students. We do not process banking payments, hold funds in escrow, or ship physical books. Transactions are agreed upon and conducted in person on campus.

3. Recommended Campus Safety Protocol
  • Always meet in public campus areas with daylight visibility (library atrium, campus dining center, or quad).
  • Thoroughly inspect the textbook before completing the handoff (check editions, notes, pages, and condition).
  • Never wire or transfer money to sellers who refuse to meet on campus.
  • Report suspicious listings or behavior immediately using the "Report" button on any listing.
4. Data Storage and Account Security

All user passwords are encrypted using industry-standard Werkzeug scrypt hashing. We do not store plain-text passwords under any circumstances. Uploaded book cover images are handled through secure cloud storage.
