The BookLoop Mission

Affordable Textbooks.
Zero Student Waste.

BookLoop was founded by students who believe academic literature should circulate freely and affordably across campuses, instead of sitting unused on dorm shelves.

Our Core Philosophy

Every semester, college students face crippling textbook expenses averaging $1,200 per academic year. Commercial bookstore buybacks pay pennies on the dollar, only to resell those same books to freshmen at astronomical prices.

BookLoop cuts out middlemen. We empower students to sell their books directly to the next cohort or trade book-for-book with peers enrolled in parallel courses. Every transaction is 100% peer-to-peer with zero commission fees.

Zero Commission

100% of purchase money stays between the students who agree on the deal.

True Course Swaps

Exchange your completed term textbooks directly for next semester's reading list.

Verified Campus Meetups

Safe, daylight meetups at college libraries, quad lawns, and student unions.

Frequently Asked Questions

BookLoop is designed specifically for peer-to-peer campus communities. When a seller accepts your purchase or exchange request, contact information is revealed so you can meet up on campus. Payment is completed in person (cash, campus card, or mutually agreed peer apps) after inspecting the book.

To propose an exchange, you must have at least one book listed in your account. When proposing an exchange, you select which of your books you want to offer in trade. The owner can review your book's condition and choose to accept or decline the swap.
